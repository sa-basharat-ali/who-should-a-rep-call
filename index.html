Who should a rep call, and what should the brief say?

A synthetic outbound book for a restaurant software company on a flat $499/month subscription: 20,000 independent restaurants a quarter, capacity to work 5,000. Every restaurant has two hidden numbers, the chance it signs with no rep at all and the lift a rep adds. They are not the same restaurants.

The parts I got wrong

  1. I first drew the holdout only from leads the old rule skipped. The uplift model had never seen a pricing-page restaurant go unworked, so it still sent reps to 358 of them, and only 67% of its deals were rep-caused. Drawn from the whole book: 0 and 79%. The holdout has to include the leads you'd work anyway, which is exactly what makes it expensive.
  2. My first scoring of the briefs said 34% of missing fields were invented. Half were valid inferences: a record with 450 monthly app orders does use delivery apps. The real number is 18%.
